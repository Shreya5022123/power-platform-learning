**1. Microsoft Dynamics 365**
- A collection of cloud-based business applications developed by Microsoft.
- Helps organisations manage customers, sales, finance, operations and services.
- Combines CRM (Customer Relationship Management) and ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) capabilities.

CRM vs ERP
- CRM: Manages customer relationships, sales and customer service.
- ERP: Manages internal business operations such as finance, inventory and supply chain.

**2. Dynamics 365 Applications**

Applications
- Sales	Manages leads, customers and sales opportunities

- Customer Service	Manages customer queries and complaints

- Customer Insights	Customer data analysis and marketing

- Field Service	Manages field workers and service appointments

- Finance	Manages financial operations

- Supply Chain Management	Manages inventory and logistics

- Human Resources	Manages employee information

- Business Central	Business management for small and medium businesses

- Commerce	Manages retail and e-commerce

- Project Operations	Manages projects and resources

**3. Microsoft Power Platform**

A collection of low-code tools used to build applications, automate processes and analyse data.
Components
- Power Apps-	Build custom business applications
- Power Automate-	Automate workflows and repetitive tasks
- Power BI-	Data analysis and visualisation
- Power Pages-	Build external-facing websites
- Copilot Studio-	Build AI-powered conversational agents

**4. Power Platform Supporting Components**
Components
- Dataverse	-Stores and manages business data using tables
- Connectors-	Connect Power Platform with external services
- AI Builder-	Adds AI capabilities to applications and workflows
- Power Fx-	Formula language used in Power Apps
- Managed Environments-	Provides governance and administration capabilities

**5. Microsoft Dataverse**
- Microsoft's cloud-based data platform.
- Stores business data in tables, rows and columns.
- Supports relationships between tables.
- Provides security and access management.
- Used by Power Apps and Dynamics 365.
Example: Customer, Employee, Policy and Claim tables.

**6. Microsoft Azure**

- Microsoft's cloud computing platform.
- Provides infrastructure and cloud services.
- Supports application hosting, integration, security and analytics.

Capabilities
- Identity & Access Management	-Manages identities and permissions
- AI Services-	Provides AI capabilities
- Data Analytics	-Processes and analyses data
- Custom Development	-Supports custom applications
- Integration-	Connects different systems
- IoT	-Connects physical devices

**7. How These Technologies Work Together**

Example: Insurance Claim Management System.

1. Power Pages: Customer submits an insurance claim.
2. Dataverse: Stores customer and claim information.
3. Power Automate: Sends claim approval notifications.
4. Power Apps: Employees manage and update claims.
5. Dynamics 365: Manages customer service operations.
6. Outlook / Teams: Sends notifications and facilitates communication.
7. Power BI: Displays claim analytics and reports.
8. Azure: Supports custom backend services and integrations.
